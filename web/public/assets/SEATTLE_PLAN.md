# Seattle pilot and coverage plan

## Bounded question

For one identified historical parking episode under the supplied version of SMC 11.72.440, which explicit premises support or oppose the ordinary 72-hour branch, and what evidence is missing?

The pilot is deliberately bounded before expansion. The repository contains 14 source-backed rules, three authorities, four pinned artifacts (one archival mirror), five historical proof paths from one real litigation matter, and two unresolved assessment contexts. It does not measure a percentage of Seattle law: the denominator is unknown.

## Primary sources and editions

Seattle Ordinance 120573 and the Supreme Court's Timbs PDF are pinned. The official original Long slip PDF is now pinned and used by canonical rules and case facts. The older mirrored text is retained for reproducible legacy fixtures. Reconciliation with the final revised reporter text is still pending; the official slip is not presented as a final edition.

The Long event date is 2016-10-12. Governing-law selection and the 2021-08-13 authority context are separate. Recording timestamps reflect this corpus's ingestion. These choices are reviewable assumptions.

## Acceptance criteria

Before wider use, obtain independent legal and formalization reviews tied to the exact snapshot; reconcile the source edition; audit each premise against its pinpoint; identify omitted exceptions and alternative readings; and compare the workbench with a lawyer-written checklist on multiple held-out matters.

`pilot.json` records reviewer time, agreement, critical omissions, and checklist comparison as null until measured. `review.json` keeps both reviews pending. `review-packet.json` supplies 14 source mappings, assumptions, omissions, and review questions. Generating the packet is not a completed review.

## What remains unmodeled

One Matter identifies one issue or parking episode. Multi-vehicle and multi-episode aggregation, procedural posture, burdens, remedies, analogical precedent, conflicts of laws, full preemption, and subsequent treatment are not automatically resolved. Absence of a modeled exception is not evidence that no exception exists.

Quantity units are exact tags; there are no implicit conversions or calendar rules. A classifier probability is not a legal burden of proof. Oracle credentials and review identities require external authentication.

## Expansion

Expand only after the pilot's omissions and review costs are measured. Add new issue modules with distinct sources and expert-reviewed authority policies. Municipal nesting must not silently encode legal priority. Other countries require their own legal-system conventions; US case-law assumptions are not universal.
